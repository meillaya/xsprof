    Run it yourself

    record a bounded window

    xsprof record --duration 2000 --output session.ndjson

    keep a rotating recorder

    xsprof daemon --foreground --dir /var/lib/xsprof --retain 10m --max-bytes 2G

    xsprof capture --last 5m --dir /var/lib/xsprof --out incident.ndjson

    watch over SSH

    xsprof watch --dir /var/lib/xsprof --window 30s

    Refused by design

    host_mutation=false

    • enable
    • mutate

    • setaffinity
    • setpriority
    • bind
